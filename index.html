03 — Approach

How I build

Three rules that decide most technical choices.

01

Boundaries before frameworks

Business rules live in plain classes with tests. Laravel, queues, and databases are adapters at the edge — so a swap costs a week, not a rewrite.

02

Performance is a feature

Every endpoint gets a budget. Indexes, eager loading, and caching are part of the design review, not a fire drill after launch.

03

Boring beats clever

Idempotent jobs, explicit contracts, and logs you can actually read at 3 a.m. Predictability is what lets a small team move fast.
